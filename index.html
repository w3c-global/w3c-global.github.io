01 · The barrier

AI can run the operation. It cannot yet prove it.

Institutions already let AI advise, forecast and draft. Letting it move real money on its own is a different question entirely. The moment an agent touches a ledger, someone has to answer for what it did: whether the action was correct, whether it was tampered with, whether it can be reconstructed on demand for an auditor or a regulator. Until that answer is always yes, the operations that matter most stay closed to AI.

02 · How Agentu works

Trust, built into every action.

Agentu does not ask you to take AI on faith. It makes every operation provable as it happens.

✓ Recorded 01

Auditable

Every AI action is recorded the instant it happens, with a complete trail you can inspect, export and prove. Instantly auditable, by design.

✓ Real time 02

Monitored

Agentu watches every operation continuously, as it runs. Nothing executes unobserved, and nothing slips through unrecorded.

✓ Assessed 03

Accountable

Every action is judged and assessed as it executes. Your AI does not just act. It answers for what it did.

Immutable by default

A record that cannot be rewritten.

Once an action is written to Agentu it cannot be altered, reordered or deleted. Every entry is sealed and tamper-evident the instant it is recorded, which makes the ledger far harder to change after the fact than the mutable databases and core systems that most banking infrastructure still runs on. What happened stays exactly as it happened, for you, your auditors and your regulators.
