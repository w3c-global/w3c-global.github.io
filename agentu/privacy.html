Privacy and development data

Agentu is a pre-incorporation venture. This site presents the project and a demonstration of financial controls. It does not provide banking or payment services.

Enquiries

The enquiry form prepares an email to frankie@w3c.com in your own email application. You choose whether to send it. The website does not submit or store the form contents. Your email provider and the recipient’s email service handle messages you send.

Demonstration

Use fictional business names and simulated transactions only. Do not enter bank credentials, card details or information about real customers. In the hosted demo, sign-in and session records are held in the dedicated AWS environment. Demo sessions are scheduled for expiry after seven days; expiry is asynchronous. Infrastructure backups and security logs may remain for longer. The local rehearsal keeps its demo state in a local file until that file is removed.

Browser storage and external resources

The operations workspace stores institution names, account records, membership emails, policies, proposed actions, decisions and ledger entries. Use fictional institutions and simulated funds during development. Platform records are retained until an authorised retention process removes them; they do not use the demonstration's seven-day expiry. The local workspace stores these records and hashed development passwords in a local database. Local email ownership is assumed for testing. The local sign-in cookie expires after eight hours or on sign-out.

The hosted applications use session storage for short-lived sign-in tokens. The operations workspace also keeps its selected institution and pending invitation in session storage. Unconfirmed submission keys and a hash of the request are retained in local storage so retries can avoid duplicate postings, including after sign-in again. These keys contain no passwords, invitation tokens or request contents and are removed when the request has a definitive result. Signing out clears access to the workspace. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the usual browser request data. No advertising or behavioural analytics have been added. Hosting providers may retain access and security logs.

Contact

For questions about this project or to request removal of an enquiry or identifiable demo record, contact frankie@w3c.com. A full operating-company privacy notice will be established before a customer service launches.
