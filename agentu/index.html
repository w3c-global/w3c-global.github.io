01 / The control layer

Before money moves,
the rules should speak.

Financial operations need more than an agent’s answer. They need a defined mandate, a decision you can explain, and a record you can inspect. Agentu is being developed to bring those controls into the action itself.

01

Propose

An agent requests an action, with a purpose, amount and destination.

02

Check

Policy checks test the mandate, destination and available liquidity.

03

Decide

Allow the action, block it, or hold it for a person’s approval.

04

Record

Keep the request, decision and outcome together in an exportable audit trail.

03 / The network ambition

A shared standard
for accountable action.

The ambition is a network through which institutions can operate AI under common controls, with evidence that travels with each action.

Banking, payments and treasury are the starting points. The proposed participant model includes activity-based revenue sharing; commercial terms and integrations will be developed with future partners.
